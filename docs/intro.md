# Fanar

Fanar connects to your data, answers questions about it in plain language, and
turns those answers into queries, charts and dashboards your team can share.

You can drive it two ways, and most teams use both:

- **Ask.** Type a question and get an answer, a chart, or a whole dashboard —
  with the SQL shown so you can check it.
- **Build.** Write SQL directly in the query editor, visualize it, schedule it,
  and alert on it.

## Getting the most out of the AI

Fanar knows your schema. It does not know your company's definitions until you tell
it. If answers are close but not right,
[Teaching Fanar your business](./ask/teaching-fanar) is where to look first — it's
the highest-return page on this site.
