---
title: "Adding a Profile Picture"
---

If you login to Fanar with a Google account, Fanar will automatically fetch your Google Account profile picture. Alternatively, Fanar uses [Gravatar](https://en.gravatar.com/) to handle profile pictures.

If you don't have a Gravatar account, sign up for Gravatar with the email address you used to sign-in to Fanar.

Gravatars are cached by your browser. Once signed up or updated, your Gravatar will appear on Fanar within 5 minutes after the change following a page refresh.
